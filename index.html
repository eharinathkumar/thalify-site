What's inside

More than a number at the bottom of the day.

Thalify plans, coaches, and remembers — the parts most trackers leave to you.

AI meal logging

Type or speak a whole meal. It splits into items with calories, protein, carbs and fat — cooking oil included, portions understood.

A plan that adapts

Set a goal weight and Thalify sets your targets and a realistic pace, then adjusts them at a weekly check-in based on your real progress.

A coach that remembers

Each week it reads how you did, nudges one habit, and keeps notes on what works for you — so guidance gets more personal over time.

Instant food lookup

Thousands of foods built in. Can't find one? Ask the AI and it works out the nutrition — then saves it to your list for next time.

Health & weight logs

Track weight over time, and optionally log blood sugar, HbA1c or blood pressure with your own reminder intervals. Readings stay on your device.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your diary lives in your phone's own storage — there's no server holding your data.
