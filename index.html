Not just counting

One plan. Checked in on every week.

Tell Thalify your goal weight and how you eat. It works out your calorie and protein targets, sets a realistic pace, and gives you a handful of habits that fit your kitchen — not someone else's.

Privacy

Your food diary never leaves your phone.

There's no Thalify account and no Thalify database. Your meals, weight and profile live in your phone's own storage. When you use an AI feature, only that request — the sentence you typed, or the numbers needed to build a plan — is sent to be answered, and it isn't stored or tied to you.

No sign-up No ads No tracking No data sold Export anytime
Who made this

Built in a kitchen, not a boardroom.

I'm an engineer, and I got tired of watching my family guess. My wife would log "1 serving curry" and shrug. My parents in India had no app that spoke their food at all. So I built the tracker I wanted us to have — one that knows a kuzhambu from a korma, and knows we eat Chipotle on Thursdays too.

It's free to use. There's no company behind it and nothing to upsell you. If it helps you eat a little better, that's the whole point.
